# A quiet decision

Morning light fills the empty industrial cafe; the espresso counter is left and the window tables are right.

Andre sits at the round window table. A folded cream note rests before him. He lowers his gaze toward it.

The folded note lies still on the wooden tabletop, without readable writing. No hands enter the frame.

Andre pauses, then raises his gaze toward the window with his lips closed.

From the reverse-counter view, Andre holds his gaze toward the daylight; his body remains still.

The wide cafe view returns. Andre gives one slight nod at the same window table and remains seated.
